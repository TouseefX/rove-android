# terminal artwork

The Rove icon in `assets/rove.webp` was supplied by the project owner from
[the Rove Discord server](https://cdn.discordapp.com/icons/1567007730764353616/ef2eb95e10c66b7e3d84443d024935f8.webp?size=2048).
The downloaded original is bundled without changing its colors or geometry.

Game thumbnails are supplied by Roblox's public thumbnail service and belong
to their respective owners. They are loaded on demand, kept in a bounded
memory cache, and are not bundled with this application.

Terminal image protocols are encoded by
[ratatui-image](https://github.com/ratatui/ratatui-image) (MIT).
